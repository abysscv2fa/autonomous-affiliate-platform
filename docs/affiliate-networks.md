# Affiliate network shortlist (US / UK / Europe)

This is a practical starting shortlist, not a guarantee of acceptance. A publisher account and each advertiser/program application may be reviewed separately. Confirm eligible countries, traffic methods, payout method, tax forms, cookie attribution, commission, reversals and brand restrictions in the live terms before promoting anything.

| Network | Best initial use | Cost / access notes | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| PartnerStack | B2B SaaS, software and recurring partner programs | Apply as a partner; individual vendor programs have their own approval and terms | https://partnerstack.com/our-partner-program/partners-affiliate |
| Awin | UK and European retail/ecommerce brands; also broader global advertiser coverage | Check current country signup terms | https://www.awin.com/gb/pricing/affiliate-partners |
| CJ Affiliate | Established advertisers across US and international markets | Publisher signup is free; advertiser-specific approval applies | https://www.cj.com/join |
| Amazon Associates | Physical products and buyer-intent comparison content | Separate locale applications; Amazon requires a site and locale-specific tags | https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GCE3F2NCVZLDFM6A |

## Recommended order
1. Start with PartnerStack for software/SaaS because the content angle can focus on recurring business problems and software comparisons.
2. Apply to Awin for UK/EU retail coverage.
3. Apply to CJ for US/international brand coverage after the site has useful original content.
4. Add Amazon only when the site has product-review and comparison content that can satisfy the local program's rules.

## Publishing and compliance gates
- Verify applicant eligibility and payment support from Vietnam before investing time.
- Do not fabricate product testing, customer reviews, features, prices, conversion rates or commission terms.
- Place a clear affiliate disclosure close to recommendations and links.
- Use original, useful comparisons and first-hand evidence where possible; avoid scaled low-value AI pages and thin affiliate content.
- Do not scrape sites against their terms, use automated clicks, traffic exchanges, cookie stuffing, unsolicited bulk email or fake reviews.
